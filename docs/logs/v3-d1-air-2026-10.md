### Findings

1. `RelTier .d1 = RelD1` is unbounded: no A1/A2/Canon0f/A7–A10/A12, and no bound on the transaction
   count (failed transactions burn 0 gas; ≈ 49 k fit an 8 MiB witness). A bounded tier `D1a` is
   required for any succinct prover; the D0a predicates decode with the D0 witness decoder, so each
   needs a D1 restatement (design §3).
2. `transactions` are bound to the claim by `slotB2.txRoot`, and steps 1–4 of `processTx` (dedup,
   expiry flag, `sizeOk ∧ Ed25519.verify`, `txCost`) and all outcome/receipt construction are
   state-free: class H. A native verifier (`prepD1`) with spec functions removes Ed25519/SHA-512
   from the AIR entirely (design §2, PROPOSAL P-D1-2). The remaining in-AIR delta is two small
   tables (`txV3`, `akmV3`), three first-row offset edits in `rcptV3`, and public segments; 23 of 25
   tables are reused unchanged.
3. `decodeAccessKey = none` rejects (not "fails"), so the AIR must prove FunctionCall / gas-key
   values decode (UTF-8 strings): an `akmV3` decoder (recommended) or a hint, or amendment T4 (not
   recommended: it drops `fc_key_*`/`gas_key_v0` classes the spec places inside D1).
4. Local receipt bytes are not witness data: hashing them in `rcptV3` against a native digest would
   need SHA-256 collision resistance. Their only state-dependent part is the signer credit, so it is
   a `txV3` op; outcome/refund/id/tokens are native once the success bits are public.

### Prototype: in-AIR Ed25519 cost spike (`examples/d1-air-spike/ed25519-cost`)

Standalone, dependency-free Rust (own 256/512-bit arithmetic and SHA-512). Two schedules over all
7,265 signature vectors of `oracle/fixtures/v3/ed25519` against nearcore's `verify_raw` verdict:

* faithful (exact `NearSpecV3.Ed25519` operation sequence): **7,265/7,265 agree**; var×var muls
  mean 6,122, **max 7,469**; const muls mean 1,293; 646 point additions;
* optimized (hinted roots / non-square witness via √−1, 64×16 fixed-base comb with affine-Niels
  madds, 4-bit window, dedicated doubling, hinted Z⁻¹): **7,265/7,265 agree**; muls mean 2,655,
  **max 3,095**;
* 32×8-bit limb gadget (2²⁵⁶ ≡ 38 fold, signed carry chain): 1,355,312 products from the first 200
  vectors checked over ℤ, in BabyBear and against the reference; worst case (all limbs 2⁹−1, plus
  100,000 random) max coefficient 307,861,659 ≈ 2^28.20 < p_BB/2 ≈ 2^29.9, max carry ≈ 2^20.2
  (21-bit range checks);
* SHA-512: 503/503 hashlib vectors; Rust reference verify 117 µs (faithful) / 59 µs (optimized),
  one core.

Evidence: `docs/e2e-results/v3-d1-air/ed25519-cost.json` (2.6 s, CPU 10).

### Cost model / formal effort (summary; details in the design)

* D1-N at the proposed bounds (NT = 4,096, A1, B1 = 2 MB): prove ≈ D0a + 10–20 s, memory ≈ D0a,
  STARK bound ≈ 6.6 MB + hint ≤ 3.7 MB, verify + ≤ 14 s (spec Ed25519 at 3.4 ms) or ≈ 1–3 s with a
  fast path. On the public-d1 fixtures: ≈ D0a's 263–312 s plus < 5 s.
* D1-S faithful: ≈ 3.6 M W-eq cells / signature (≈ 0.27 s at the D0a-calibrated 75 s per G W-eq
  cells); ≈ 530 signatures per 2²² gadget table; over the cloud prove budget and 16 GiB at ≈ 500
  signatures without prover rework (honest instead of fixed 2²² heights, memory schedule).
* Formal: D1-N ≈ 45–90 k new Lean lines; combined D1a closure projected 10,000–12,500 s
  single-core of the 14,400 s elaboration budget: apply the codec_int lever (3,285 s, 38 % of
  D0a's closure) first; keep the D0a artifact separate.
